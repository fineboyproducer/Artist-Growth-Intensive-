The Problem Isn't Always Effort

Artists are constantly told to do more.

Post more. Release more. Promote more. Spend more. Network more. Do more. But doing more doesn't automatically mean you're moving forward. The real question is — are you doing the right things, and do you know why you're doing them?

With a strategy
